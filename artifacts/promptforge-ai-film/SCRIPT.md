# PromptForge AI — Animated Intro

## Director's treatment
- **Promise:** A rough thought can become a clear, ready-to-use prompt without leaving your device.
- **Identity:** PromptForge's deep ink workspace, lavender-to-cobalt tool accent, mint privacy signal; Space Grotesk display with DM Sans support and JetBrains Mono annotations.
- **Hero:** One luminous, editable-looking thought fragment that is physically forged into a structured prompt.
- **Motion:** Macro type scale-through, split-panel assembly, and a radial violet aperture that resolves into a clean wordmark.
- **Arc:** Start intimate and uncertain, accelerate through the act of shaping, then land in a calm, confident product proof and a distinct brand lockup.
- **Payoff:** The final lockup is a quiet cream-on-ink inversion of the active violet workshop, with the privacy promise readable and the opening thought fragment returning as a loop carrier.
- **Format:** 16:9 landscape.
- **Runtime:** 24.00 seconds.

## Fully scripted shots

### Shot 01 / 00.00–03.75 / 3.75s — “A THOUGHT.”
**Purpose:** Make the user's raw starting point feel human and unfinished, rather than a failed input.

**Exact content:** The lowercase phrase `launch email` appears at 00.25s, initially letter-spaced and incomplete. `ROUGH IS RIGHT` appears at 01.05s. At 02.00s the phrase briefly separates into `launch` and `email`; at 03.02s the supporting line `Start anywhere.` appears and holds through 03.48s. No voiceover.

**Composition:** Ink (#0b0e15) field with a faintly gridded violet plane, tight crop on `launch email` at the right-center, 26vmin type. Upper-left safe zone holds `ROUGH IS RIGHT` at 2vmin. One thin electric-violet cursor rule crosses from lower-left to the word; no other copy occupies that region. Cool keylight, restrained lavender edge light.

**Artistic idea:** The cursor is a chisel. The raw phrase is a massive physical wordform whose kerning is visibly imperfect; each hit snaps a letter into a steadier rhythm.

**Choreography:** 00.00–00.22s: opening frame already has the cursor and a cropped letterform in view. 00.25s: `launch` punches into focus. 00.72s: `email` snaps on, 0.47s after first hit. 01.05s: eyebrow stamps in as the whole phrase snaps to a stable baseline. 01.62s: cursor rule travels under the words and catches an accent. 02.00s: the two words separate by 2vmin, then settle by 02.35s. 03.02s: supporting line resolves. At 03.48s the cursor rule lengthens and pushes through lens, carrying into the next macro rule.

**Transition contract:** The outgoing cursor rule becomes the long horizontal separator in shot 02. The departing phrase shears upward with a slight blur while an incoming violet split panel unfolds in the same direction. Both overlap for 0.28s; the 03.75s cut lands on a measured musical onset.

**Assets/technique:** Typography and CSS/SVG grid, cursor rule, masked letterforms; no external image assets.

**Sound intent:** Instrumental electronic bed; 03.75s cut matches a measured onset.

**Artistic idea:** Prompt structure is made from three real, rigid rails that shear, rotate and dock into one aligned system. The raw cursor becomes the exact separator rail.

**Transition contract:** The center bracket is a persistent physical carrier. It scales to a rounded prompt frame while the three rail labels fragment and exit toward their corresponding zones. Shot 03 opens with the same frame edge, plus an incoming left panel and a color-plane flip; the cut at 08.10s matches a measured onset.

**Assets/technique:** CSS panels and vector rules, 3D perspective transforms, SVG label ticks, masked outline type. No external imagery.

**Sound intent:** Cut at 08.10s matches a measured onset.

## Composition review
Five adjacent shots vary in at least two of field system, scale, layout, and mechanic. Action escalates from intimate macro word shaping, to rigid panel assembly, to multi-plane transformation, to a readable product sheet, then to the inverted text-only finale. The product UI is truthful to the source app: it is a browser-local template utility, not a model call. Each shot has beats at least once per second and begins its outgoing handoff in its last 15–20%.
